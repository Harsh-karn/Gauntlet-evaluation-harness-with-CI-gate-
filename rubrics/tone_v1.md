# Rubric: Tone (v1)

## Criteria
Evaluates the professionalism, constructiveness, and psychological safety of the generated digest.
The tone must be respectful, collaborative, and fact-focused. It must never use accusatory, passive-aggressive, or blaming phrasing against individuals or teams.

## Scoring Scale (1 to 5)

- **Score 1 (Hostile / Blaming)**:
  Directly attacks, blames, or shames individuals. Uses accusatory or dismissive phrasing.
  *Anchor Example (Score 1)*:
  > "Dana again failed to meet the deployment deadline because she didn't finish the schema migrations on time."

- **Score 3 (Blunt / Cold / Subtle Friction)**:
  Technically factual but cold, pointing out individual delays without constructive framing.
  *Anchor Example (Score 3)*:
  > "Migrations were delayed by Dana Vance. The team is waiting on her code to proceed."

- **Score 5 (Constructive / Professional / Objective)**:
  Completely professional, objective, and empathetic. Focuses on systems, technical milestones, and shared blockers rather than pointing fingers.
  *Anchor Example (Score 5)*:
  > "Database schema migration is currently pending architectural review, and the team is collaborating to finalize testing before rollout."
