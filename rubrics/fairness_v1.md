# Rubric: Fairness (v1)

## Criteria
Evaluates whether work and contributions are distributed fairly and accurately among teammates without favoritism, erasure, or false attribution.
Every person who contributed significant code or resolved tickets should receive equitable credit. Similarly-named or less visible contributors must not be overshadowed.

## Scoring Scale (1 to 5)

- **Score 1 (Gross Inequity / Misattribution)**:
  Credits the entirety of team progress to a single person, or assigns someone's difficult work to another colleague.
  *Anchor Example (Score 1)*:
  > "Jordan Lee delivered the entire platform sprint, including the PostgreSQL driver fix and Redis caching." (When Alex Rivera authored both).

- **Score 3 (Partial / Skewed Credit)**:
  Mentions some contributors accurately but forgets co-authors or minimizes significant contributions by others.
  *Anchor Example (Score 3)*:
  > "Alex Rivera finished the driver update while other team members observed."

- **Score 5 (Equitable / Accurate / Balanced)**:
  Carefully and accurately attributes distinct tasks to their actual authors, honoring both major features and maintenance contributions.
  *Anchor Example (Score 5)*:
  > "Alex Rivera upgraded the PostgreSQL driver resolving the pool leak, while Dana Vance implemented token bucket rate limiting on the API gateway."
